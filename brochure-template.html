AI Digital Twin Platform — Product Overview Page 2
Twin
Control & Oversight
You are always the last word.
Every AI draft resolves to exactly one of four explicit human decisions — nothing is silently auto-approved or auto-sent.
Approve
The draft is sent exactly as written.
Edit & Approve
You refine the draft, then send your version.
Reject
The draft is discarded — nothing is sent.
Escalate
Routed to a senior reviewer for judgment.
Measure what the AI is actually doing
A built-in eval dashboard tracks approval rate, edit rate, and quality over time — so trust in the system is based on evidence, not assumption.
Approval Rate
Share of AI drafts approved as-is, tracked over time and by workflow type.
Edit Rate
How often a draft needed a human correction before it was usable.
Quality Tags
Reviewer-rated Good / Needs Edit / Bad on every decision made.
Enforced guardrails — not a suggestion, a policy
Every AI draft is checked against a fixed, non-negotiable policy before it ever reaches a reviewer. Flagged examples include:
Max 15% discount without approval "we guarantee" "legally binding" "100% guaranteed" "unconditional refund" "free forever" "unlimited liability" "auto-renew"
Full decision history
Every approval, edit, and rejection is logged and reviewable.
Nothing acts without you
No auto-send, no silent execution — ever, by design.
Evidence-based trust
Approval and edit rates make AI performance measurable, not assumed.
